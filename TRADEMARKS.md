# Name and logo policy

<p align="center">

The software license and project identity are separate things.

AGPL-3.0-only grants rights in the covered software. It does not grant a right to present a modified fork, service or distribution as an official Polymorph release or as endorsed by Angus Uelsmann.

This policy does not claim that every project name, logo or mark is formally registered. It explains how the Polymorph project identity may be used without creating confusion about origin or endorsement.

## Fine

You may:

- refer to Polymorph by name when accurately describing the software
- say that a product or fork is based on, derived from or compatible with Polymorph when that statement is true
- link to the canonical repository or [angusu.de](https://angusu.de)
- retain required copyright, license and provenance notices
- use the name in technical discussion, reviews, documentation and factual comparisons

## Please use a different identity for modified products

If you distribute or operate a materially modified version, use a distinct product name and visual identity unless a separate agreement says otherwise.

Do not use the Polymorph name or logo in a way that reasonably suggests that a modified fork, hosted service or commercial product is the official project, is maintained by Angus Uelsmann or is endorsed by him when it is not.

A fork may say "based on Polymorph". Calling the fork simply "Polymorph" while changing the product behind it is considerably less helpful. Names are supposed to reduce ambiguity too.

## Logo

The project logo may be used when linking to or accurately identifying the official Polymorph project.

Do not use it as the primary brand of an unofficial modified product or service without permission.

## Commercial branding

White-label, co-branding or other negotiated branding rights may be included in a separate commercial agreement. See [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md).

## No change to software rights

This policy is about project identity. It does not remove or narrow rights granted under [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), and it is not an additional software-license condition.

For licensing questions, see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).
