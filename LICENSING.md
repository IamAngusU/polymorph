The short version: **public source is not the same thing as unrestricted use.**

Polymorph is source-available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). It is not OSI Open Source.

The actual permissions and conditions are defined by [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). This page explains the repository's licensing structure in normal human language. If this summary and the license ever disagree, the license wins. It is the one wearing the legal shoes.

## Public noncommercial use

The public repository license is PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0.

Use that license itself to determine whether a specific use is permitted. Required Notice lines in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) must remain with redistributed copies where the license requires them.

This repository does not add broader commercial permissions merely because the source is visible, cloneable or technically easy to put into a container.

## Commercial use

Commercial use is not granted by the public repository license.

A separate written commercial license is available directly from Angus Uelsmann. Depending on the use case, commercial terms may cover things such as:

- internal business use
- embedding Polymorph into another product
- hosted or managed services
- redistribution
- white-label use
- revenue participation or other negotiated commercial terms

Those rights exist only when they are agreed in a separate written agreement. A procurement email, a Git clone and optimism do not accidentally form a license.

See [Commercial licensing](COMMERCIAL.md).

## Contributions

The project may use separate commercial licenses in addition to the public noncommercial license. That means third-party code cannot simply be merged under an incompatible assumption about downstream rights.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before sending code.

## Third-party components

Third-party components retain their own licenses and terms. See [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md).

Optional model profiles may also have upstream dataset or weight-license restrictions beyond the repository license. Their provenance is documented separately under [Model profiles](docs/MODEL_PROFILE.md).
